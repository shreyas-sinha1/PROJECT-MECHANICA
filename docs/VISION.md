# Project Mechanica — Product Vision

## Vision

Project Mechanica exists to make physics more intuitive by helping students
understand the physical mechanisms behind physics, rather than relying only
on rote learning, formula memorization, and numerical substitution.

The goal is not to replace physics education or teach physics from scratch.

The goal is to make the invisible physical process visible.

---

## The Problem

A student can solve a large number of physics problems while still failing to
develop an intuitive understanding of what is physically happening.

Consider a student preparing for JEE or a similar physics-intensive examination.

Suppose a student practices approximately 50 physics questions every day for
2.5 years.

Even using an intentionally conservative assumption, suppose only 10 questions
per day are not properly understood.

That represents thousands of opportunities over the student's preparation where
the student encounters something they cannot fully reason about.

Some of these difficulties are genuine conceptual gaps.

However, many problems arise because the student cannot visualize the physical
situation.

They may know the relevant formulas and principles, but cannot intuitively see:

- what is physically happening,
- how the components are interacting,
- how the mechanism evolves,
- why the forces or motions behave the way they do,
- or how the abstract mathematical description corresponds to the physical
  world.

This gap between mathematical representation and physical intuition is where
Project Mechanica operates.

---

## What Mechanica Does

Mechanica does not primarily teach physics from first principles.

Instead, it visualizes the physical mechanism underlying a physics problem or
concept.

It shows the student what is actually happening in the physical world.

The student should be able to see the mechanism operating rather than merely
read an explanation of it.

For example, instead of simply presenting equations describing a system,
Mechanica should help the student observe the relevant motion, forces,
interactions, constraints, trajectories, and changes occurring within that
system.

The visualization is intended to provide the missing intuition.

---

## Target Audience

The primary audience is students studying physics, ranging from:

- High-school physics
- Competitive-examination physics
- Undergraduate and university-level physics

Mechanica is particularly intended for students who genuinely want to
understand the mechanisms behind physics rather than simply memorize formulas,
substitute numbers, and obtain answers.

---

## Core Belief

Physics becomes substantially more intuitive when mathematical descriptions
can be connected to a visible physical mechanism.

Project Mechanica therefore focuses on the question:

> "What is actually happening?"

rather than only:

> "Which formula should I use?"

---

## Product Principle

Mechanica should not overwhelm the student with another layer of theory.

It should reveal the mechanism.

The product should allow the student to move from:

**Problem → Physical situation → Mechanism → Intuition → Understanding**

rather than:

**Problem → Formula → Substitution → Answer**

---

## The Desired Outcome

After interacting with a Mechanica visualization, the student should not merely
remember how to solve the particular problem.

They should have a clearer mental model of the physical system.

The objective is therefore not simply to help students obtain more answers.

It is to help them develop **physical intuition**.
